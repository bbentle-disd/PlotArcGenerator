Classroom tool based on Kurt Vonnegut's “Shapes of Stories” idea. The five comparison shapes here are simplified classroom approximations, not mathematical formulas. A story can combine or resist shapes. The goal is to make plot movement visible and discuss evidence. Student tabs persist locally; Canvas-account syncing and a live cross-student teacher dashboard require LTI/server support.
